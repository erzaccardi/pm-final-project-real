Future State Journey: Curated Streaming

Restore brand authority and win back users by prioritizing human-led curation over mass-market scale.

PersonaYoung professional in her late 20s who pays for her own subscription and watches casually on weeknights.
GoalLand on something she'll enjoy within a few minutes, so every subscription dollar feels worth it.
Value propCurated streaming delivers a more personalized experience that wins her preference regularly, which reduces churn and builds loyalty.
