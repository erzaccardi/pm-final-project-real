# Feature Roadmap, Module 4 · StreamLine Spotlight

**Team:** 2 engineers + 1 designer

## Strategic anchors
- **Persona:** Young professional
- **Primary metric:** 30-minute+ viewing session
- **Moment of misery:** Not being able to find something appealing, decision fatigue and orientation fatigue
- **Guardrail:** Browse-state engagement of 3+ min

## Scoring
| Feature | Value | Effort | Quadrant | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 Spotlight Curated Rail | 3 | 1 | Fill-In | Now | Medium value because without the personalization this feature might reinfforce the company promoting random things vs personalized things. Tho it does help with quick visual orientation scaffolding that will make the personalized work more noticable  |
| A2 'Why You'll Love This' Label | 4 | 4 | Major Project | Next | Targets decision fatigue and signals immediately something has changed in personlized browsing experience. We are not relying on the user to poke around long enough to realize things are more personalized |
| A3 Hidden Gem Badge | 2 | 1 | Fill-In | Now | Low effort paired with a wink at a new lens at looking at the content at a glance |
| A4 Mood-Based Entry Point | 5 | 4 | Major Project | Later | Good gateway experience to keep attention and potentially lead to a selection match to the user |
| A5 Personalized Spotlight Queue | 5 | 4 | Major Project | Next | Closely aligns with the persona's pain point |
| A6 Spotlight Digest Email | 3 | 2 | Fill-In | Next | Bumped this to next because I think it can help seed fresh mindset for the user getting exposed to the new features |
| A7 Curator Profiles | 2 | 3 | Time Sinker | Cut | Not clear that this feature aligns with the core problem |

## Roadmap
### NOW, 3-week sprint
- **A1 Spotlight Curated Rail**, Medium value because without the personalization this feature might reinfforce the company promoting random things vs personalized things. Tho it does help with quick visual orientation scaffolding that will make the personalized work more noticable
- **A3 Hidden Gem Badge**, Low effort paired with a wink at a new lens at looking at the content at a glance

### NEXT, following 1-2 sprints
- **A2 'Why You'll Love This' Label**, Targets decision fatigue and signals immediately something has changed in personlized browsing experience. We are not relying on the user to poke around long enough to realize things are more personalized
- **A5 Personalized Spotlight Queue**, Closely aligns with the persona's pain point
- **A6 Spotlight Digest Email**, Bumped this to next because I think it can help seed fresh mindset for the user getting exposed to the new features

### LATER, backlog
- **A4 Mood-Based Entry Point**, Good gateway experience to keep attention and potentially lead to a selection match to the user
- **A10 Offline Download (Spotlight)**, Doesn't align to core issues for this persona but it can prevent utility frustration when we get what we want: the user using our platform as their go-to

### ✂ Cut List
- **A7 Curator Profiles**, Not clear that this feature aligns with the core problem
- **A8 Watch Party (Spotlight)**, Group viewing is not associated with pain for this persona, and coordination with other humans means experience hinges on multi human error/success.
- **A9 Advanced Filter Engine**, Lower value without some of the other work done first. On it's own, it could just reinforce some of the manual effort required to find a liked track
